How to use NiftyBot

What it does

Every {{ cfg.SCAN_INTERVAL_MIN }} minutes during market hours (09:15-15:30 IST, trading days) it checks a rotating batch of {{ cfg.SCAN_BATCH_SIZE }} NSE stocks, scores each from 0-100, and buys paper positions when a stock breaks out above its 20-day high with a good score. It never places real orders.

The score (100 points)
  • Trend (15) · EMA momentum (10) · long-term trend (10) · RSI 55-68 (10) · MACD (10)
  • Breakout above 20-day high (15) · volume vs average (15) · near 52-week high (10) · tight base (5)

SIGNAL = score ≥ {{ cfg.MIN_SIGNAL_SCORE }} and a real breakout above the 20-day high, volume at least 1.2x average, price above EMA20, and RSI between 50 and {{ cfg.RSI_OVERHEATED|int }}. WATCH = score ≥ {{ cfg.WATCH_SCORE }} but not a signal. REJECT = low score. FILTERED = price or liquidity outside your limits. NO DATA = the data source failed for that stock.

Why you may see no signals

Real breakouts with volume are rare - often zero on a given day, and always zero on weekends and NSE holidays. The Scans page shows exactly how many stocks were checked and why each one was not picked. A quiet day is a valid outcome.

When a signal arrives (Telegram)
  1. It lists price, quantity, stop-loss, Target 1 (sell half) and Target 2.
  2. The bot manages the exits automatically in paper mode: stop-loss, move stop to break-even at Target 1, trailing stop, trend-break exit, 15-session time exit.
  3. Before you ever consider real money: paper-trade for weeks and check the History page (win rate, average win vs loss).
Pages
  • Dashboard - state, today's numbers, positions, closest candidates.
  • Scans - every scan and every stock's result and reason (also saved to data/scan_details.csv).
  • Journal - narrative log of each scan.
  • Scan now - works anytime; outside market hours it is analysis-only (no trades).
  • Pause trading - stops new paper buys; open positions keep being protected.
Strategies: every buy is Intraday or Short-Term

The class is decided when the signal is generated and stored with the position. Intraday: strong volume surge (≥{{ cfg.INTRADAY_MIN_VOL_RATIO }}x), enough volatility, a fresh breakout, entered before {{ cfg.INTRADAY_ENTRY_CUTOFF.strftime('%H:%M') }}; tight stop, quick targets, forced square-off at {{ cfg.INTRADAY_SQUARE_OFF.strftime('%H:%M') }} (no overnight carry unless INTRADAY_ALLOW_CARRY). Short-Term: everything else that passes the breakout rules; about 1-4 weeks, hard limit {{ cfg.MAX_HOLD_SESSIONS }} sessions. There is no long-term class.

Capital allocation

Up to {{ cfg.MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS }} positions. Each buy gets the free cash per empty slot scaled by signal strength, never more than {{ cfg.MAX_POSITION_PCT }}% of capital, never more than the {{ cfg.RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT }}% risk budget at the stop, never more than {{ cfg.MAX_PARTICIPATION_PCT }}% of average daily turnover, and always leaving a reserve for the empty slots. If nothing qualifies, cash stays in cash.

Position states (every scan)

Continue holding · Hold with warning · Exit recommended (needs {{ cfg.EXIT_CONFIRM_SCANS }} consecutive scans with {{ cfg.EXIT_WARN_SCORE }}+ warning points) · Stop-loss triggered · Target achieved · Position closed. Hard stops always win. Every state and reason is on the Signals page.

P&L

Realized = sold trades (after costs, kept forever on Closed). Unrealized = open positions marked at the latest price. Combined = realized + unrealized. The Performance page shows the day-by-day history.

Outlook & Analyze

Outlook is a pre-market risk assessment built at {{ cfg.OUTLOOK_EVENING.strftime('%H:%M') }} and {{ cfg.OUTLOOK_MORNING.strftime('%H:%M') }}; it is a forecast with evidence and limits, never a guarantee. Analyze (search box in the top bar) gives an independent analysis of any NSE stock and never places a trade.

Telegram commands

/status /positions /pnl /closed /watchlist /today /why SYMBOL /analyze SYMBOL /outlook /scan /pause /resume /performance /health /help

Current limits

Capital Rs {{ '{:,.0f}'.format(cfg.CAPITAL) }}, risk per trade {{ cfg.RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT }}%, max {{ cfg.MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS }} positions, max {{ cfg.MAX_TRADES_PER_DAY }} new trades/day, daily loss stop {{ cfg.MAX_DAILY_LOSS_PCT }}%, entries only {{ cfg.ENTRY_START.strftime('%H:%M') }}-{{ cfg.ENTRY_END.strftime('%H:%M') }} IST. Paper results include an assumed {{ cfg.COST_PCT_PER_SIDE }}% cost per side. Past or paper performance does not guarantee future results.
