How to use NiftyBot

What it does

Every {{ cfg.SCAN_INTERVAL_MIN }} minutes during market hours (09:15-15:30 IST, trading days) it checks a rotating batch of {{ cfg.SCAN_BATCH_SIZE }} NSE stocks, scores each from 0-100, and buys paper positions when a stock breaks out above its 20-day high with a good score. It never places real orders.

The score (100 points)
  • Trend (15) · EMA momentum (10) · long-term trend (10) · RSI 55-68 (10) · MACD (10)
  • Breakout above 20-day high (15) · volume vs average (15) · near 52-week high (10) · tight base (5)

SIGNAL = score ≥ {{ cfg.MIN_SIGNAL_SCORE }} and a real breakout above the 20-day high, volume at least 1.2x average, price above EMA20, and RSI between 50 and {{ cfg.RSI_OVERHEATED|int }}. WATCH = score ≥ {{ cfg.WATCH_SCORE }} but not a signal. REJECT = low score. FILTERED = price or liquidity outside your limits. NO DATA = the data source failed for that stock.

Why you may see no signals

Real breakouts with volume are rare - often zero on a given day, and always zero on weekends and NSE holidays. The Scans page shows exactly how many stocks were checked and why each one was not picked. A quiet day is a valid outcome.

When a signal arrives (Telegram)
  1. It lists price, quantity, stop-loss, Target 1 (sell half) and Target 2.
  2. The bot manages the exits automatically in paper mode: stop-loss, move stop to break-even at Target 1, trailing stop, trend-break exit, 15-session time exit.
  3. Before you ever consider real money: paper-trade for weeks and check the History page (win rate, average win vs loss).
Pages
  • Dashboard - state, today's numbers, positions, closest candidates.
  • Scans - every scan and every stock's result and reason (also saved to data/scan_details.csv).
  • Journal - narrative log of each scan.
  • Scan now - works anytime; outside market hours it is analysis-only (no trades).
  • Pause trading - stops new paper buys; open positions keep being protected.
Telegram commands

/status /positions /watchlist /today /why SYMBOL /scan /pause /resume /performance /health /help
